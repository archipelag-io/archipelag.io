Requirements

What you need

A computer with Docker

Linux, macOS (Intel or Apple Silicon), or Windows, with Docker. An NVIDIA GPU is optional; 8 GB+ VRAM unlocks larger GPU Cargos. Without one, your Island takes CPU-compatible Cargos.

Or a phone

An iPhone or iPad on iOS 16+ for small on-device models (some Cargos need iOS 17). Mobile Islands are experimental.

Network

A stable internet connection. Placement prefers Islands in the Consumer’s region, so where you are matters more than raw bandwidth.

What happens on your machine

The Island software is a lightweight Rust binary that manages everything. It connects to the Coordinator via NATS, pulls signed container images when a Job arrives, runs them under strict sandboxing, and streams the output back.

You control when your machine is available and what you charge. The Coordinator only sends Jobs that match your reported capabilities, availability, and virtual ask.

Security

Your machine stays safe

You never run arbitrary user code. Here's what enforces that.

  • Signed images only — Cargo containers are cosign-verified before execution
  • Registry allowlist — images can only be pulled from approved registries
  • Seccomp profiles — syscall filtering matched to Cargo sandbox tier
  • No filesystem access — containers are isolated from your local filesystem
  • Resource limits — CPU, memory, and GPU time are capped per Job
  • Network-approved only — no user-submitted containers in v1

Defense in depth

The security model assumes mutual distrust. Consumers don't trust Islands with their data. Islands don't trust the code they run. The Coordinator is the authority on what's allowed.

In v1, the network only runs Cargos that we've reviewed, signed, and published. There is no path for arbitrary user code to reach your machine.

Reputation

Karma: your public track record

Every Island has a Karma score tracked by the Coordinator. It rewards sustained, reliable compute and penalizes Jobs your Island drops.

  • Starts at 0; +1 for every hour of successful compute
  • −5 for a failed Job, −3 for a timeout, −20 for a disconnect mid-Job
  • Islands need 10 Karma before completed Jobs are credited; new Islands earn Karma 1.5× faster until then
  • Karma ranks Islands on the public leaderboard

Mobile Compute

Contribute from your phone Experimental

Got a recent iPhone or iPad? The early Archipelag.io iOS app runs small language models on-device with llama.cpp and CoreML — no GPU workstation needed.

Pair your device with a QR code, and it becomes an Island on the network. Mobile Islands participate in the same Karma and virtual-credit accounting model, with device-specific safety limits.

  • On-device execution via llama.cpp and CoreML, with Coordinator-mediated dispatch
  • Pair via QR code from the web dashboard
  • Small models built for phones, such as Qwen 3.5 0.8B and Gemma E2B
Multi-Island Compute

Your Island, part of something bigger Experimental

Experimental pipeline placement explores how a model can be divided across compatible Islands. A participating machine holds a shard and processes one stage of a Job.

Phase 2 can account virtual credits in proportion to assigned work. Interfaces, performance, and placement behavior may change while the feature is validated.

  • Coordinator selects only compatible, available Island candidates
  • Model-shard caching is tested to reduce repeated startup work
  • Each stage remains subject to the Cargo's configured sandbox policy
