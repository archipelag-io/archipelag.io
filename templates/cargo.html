Approved packages for models, runtimes, and compute tasks. Availability depends on a compatible online Island.

A Cargo is a portable, signed unit of computation that runs on Islands across the Archipelag.io network.

Think of it as a shipping manifest for compute: an entry identifies an approved artifact, runtime, and resource requirements. A Consumer submits a Job, the Coordinator selects a compatible Island, and the Cargo executes inside the configured sandbox.
