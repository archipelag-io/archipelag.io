02 — The Idea

Core principles

Route locally

Jobs run on Islands near you, not in a distant data center. Less distance, faster responses.

Settle with Islands directly

Islands publish asking prices and Consumers set budgets. Phase 2 simulates settlement with virtual credits; real-money payments are not enabled.

Trust nothing

Approved Cargos use signed artifacts, registry controls, sandboxing, and resource policies. Operators still secure the underlying machine.

04 — Why Archipelag.io

Why distribute the work

Proximity routing

The Coordinator measures RTT and favors a nearby eligible Island while still considering capability, availability, policy, and Karma.

Compute exchange

Islands publish virtual asks and Consumers set budgets. During Phase 2, the Coordinator simulates a clearing amount for each Cargo type with no real-money settlement.

Data residency

Jobs favor nearby Islands. Regional placement policies restrict eligible matches and record the selected Island; they support governance without acting as a legal guarantee.

Streaming-first

LLM responses stream token-by-token over WebSocket or SSE. Image generation reports progress. No polling, no waiting for a full response.

Zero-trust Islands

The standard Island software accepts approved, signed Cargos and applies registry controls, resource limits, and seccomp profiles. Operators still secure the underlying machine.

Karma & reputation

Success, latency, and uptime contribute to Karma. The Coordinator uses that operational history as one signal when placing future Jobs.

Multi-Island compute Experimental

Experimental pipeline and fan-out modes explore how a Job can span multiple compatible Islands when one device is not enough.

Cargo Registry

Browse catalog entries across container, WASM, GGUF, ONNX, and mobile formats. Execution still depends on an approved artifact and a compatible online Island.

Mobile compute Experimental

The early iOS and Android Island apps explore on-device inference with hardware-aware safety limits and mobile runtimes.

Batch processing Experimental

Eligible inputs can fan out across available Islands, with progress tracked and results collected through one Job flow.

Workflow orchestration Experimental

Experimental DAG orchestration coordinates dependent Cargo steps and explores parallel execution across compatible Islands.

Inference caching Experimental

Experimental semantic matching can reuse eligible results for repetitive Jobs, reducing latency and duplicated compute.

A/B testing Experimental

Experimental traffic splitting records latency and result-quality signals across multiple Cargo or model variants.

Federated fine-tuning Experimental

Experimental coordination lets participating Islands train locally and aggregate model updates without collecting each Island's source dataset.

Confidential inference Security prototype

Experimental TEE-backed modes aim to keep Job payloads encrypted outside an attested execution boundary on supported hardware.

07 — Under the Hood

Built on boring, reliable technology

No blockchain, no token. Just battle-tested infrastructure.

One request, one dependable path: coordinate, dispatch, execute, stream back.

01 Control plane Elixir / Phoenix

The Coordinator handles placement, accounting, and health. Elixir supervision isolates failures and keeps concurrent Job state manageable.

02 Messaging NATS JetStream

NATS carries Job dispatch and streamed output. JetStream acknowledgements support retry and requeue when an Island disappears.

03 Island software Rust / Tokio

A lightweight binary that manages containers via Bollard, reports heartbeats, and streams output. Compiles to a single static binary.
