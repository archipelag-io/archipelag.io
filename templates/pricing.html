Who pays what, today

Consumers
Pay nothing. Jobs are charged in virtual credits with no cash value. No payment method is collected.
Island operators
Earn virtual credits, not money. Each completed Job records its virtual clearing amount. Payouts are not enabled.
Cargo publishers
See virtual attribution. Executions of approved Cargos are tracked in the developer dashboard; no revenue share is paid.

Balances may be refilled, adjusted, or reset while metering is validated. The status page is the source of truth for when any of this changes.

FAQ

Common questions

How does jurisdiction routing work?

Attach a regional policy to a Job and the Coordinator restricts eligible placement to Islands reporting a location within that policy. The selected Island is recorded. This is a technical placement control, not a legal guarantee.

What are verification levels?

Identity verification is not required to run an Island during Phase 2. Hardware capabilities are reported by the Island software, while Karma records hours of successful compute, minus failures, timeouts, and disconnects. Audited facility tiers are not currently offered.

Is Archipelag.io GDPR compliant?

Archipelag.io provides regional placement controls and execution records that may support a GDPR program. Organizations remain responsible for assessing their own processing, contracts, and legal obligations.

Are you SOC 2 certified?

No. Archipelag.io is not currently SOC 2 certified. Design partners can discuss current security documentation and requirements directly with the team.

How does this help with the EU AI Act?

Archipelag.io can expose placement and execution information, but does not currently provide AI Act certification or a complete regulatory toolkit.

Is the beta really free?

Yes. Phase 2 uses virtual credits — no real money changes hands. Nobody pays, nobody gets paid. Test balances may be refilled, adjusted, or reset while accounting is validated.

How will pricing work after the beta?

The intended model is market-based: Islands publish asks, Consumers set budgets, and the Compute Exchange determines a clearing amount per Cargo type. Commercial pricing, payment methods, and terms have not been finalized.

Do Phase 2 credits carry over?

Not necessarily. Phase 2 balances are test data and may be refilled, adjusted, or reset. They have no cash value and do not represent a future paid balance.

Do I need to verify my identity?

Browsing public pages and the Cargo Registry does not require identity verification. Higher-trust actions can require verification depending on the feature and its Phase 2 rollout; the verification flow explains what is collected before submission.
